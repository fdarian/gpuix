`position: "sticky"` with `top`, `right`, `bottom` and `left`. A sticky element lays out like a relative one, then stays inside the scrollport of its nearest scroll container, inset by its own insets, without leaving its parent's content box, so a header sticks while its section is in view and is pushed out as the section ends.
